The script contains 109 words. Its theoretical pace is 145 WPM over 45 seconds, but only 42.5 seconds are available if the end card plays clean, raising the required pace to about 154 WPM. Natural pauses around "First," "Second," and the MFA examples make the practical read slightly longer.

Neural samples are not generated yet because Azure Speech and Foundry TTS are unavailable in the live preflight. The exact live Azure catalog must be checked before final voice IDs are promised.

**Performance:** colleague-to-colleague, less formal, with a slight lift on the opening question and a firm CTA

Best if the video is intended for broad employee education rather than identity specialists.

## Proof line

This line tests technical pronunciation, sentence length, and whether the voice can stay natural at the target tempo.
