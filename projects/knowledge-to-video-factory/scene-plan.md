# Scene Plan — Knowledge-to-Video Factory

Full runtime: 60.0s exactly (57.5s narrated + 2.5s silent end card). No music.
Captions: static style, on. Aspect: 1920x1080. Art direction: see `art-direction.json`.

Per Rule 3b: every scene below is hand-stitched from primitives (Fluent PNG
icon layers + text + shape layers), not a finished catalog design component.
No scene repeats the previous scene's dominant technique.

## s1-hook — 6.5s

- **Technique:** kinetic-type word build over an unfolding document icon.
- **Primary visual:** `Document Text.png` enters center, then `Book Open.png`
  and `Chat Help.png` spring in around it as the hook line names "trusted
  knowledge"; resolves into `Filmstrip Play.png` on "see and understand."
- **Layout:** centered hero, asymmetric drift outward on resolve.
- **Narration:** Line 1 (6.5s).
- **Caption:** on.

## s2-factory — 8.5s

- **Technique:** constellation hub — three source icons wire into one central mark.
- **Primary visual:** `Book.png` (Learn), `Document Text.png` (documentation),
  `Chat Help.png` (support) arranged as a wide triangle; hairline connectors
  draw toward a centered "Knowledge-to-Video Factory" wordmark; resolves to
  `Filmstrip Play.png` emitted from the hub.
- **Layout:** constellation, hub dead-center.
- **Narration:** Line 2 (8.5s).
- **Caption:** on.

## s3-agents — 9s

- **Technique:** state-mutation checklist — agent inspects a document, checks resolve in sequence.
- **Primary visual:** `Agents.png` beside `Document Search.png` on the left
  (asymmetric 60/40); three spine labels reveal in sequence on the right;
  `Shield Checkmark.png` and `Text Grammar Checkmark.png` replace pending
  markers one at a time as narration names accuracy.
- **Layout:** asymmetric 60/40.
- **Narration:** Line 3 (9s).
- **Caption:** on.

## s4-production — 10s

- **Technique:** full-width process rail — three stage icons assemble left to right.
- **Primary visual:** `Script.png` → `Slide Content.png` → `Mic Sparkle.png`
  travel onto a full-width rail in the order they are named; a thin connector
  line extends behind each new arrival.
- **Layout:** full-width strip.
- **Narration:** Line 4 (10s).
- **Caption:** on.

## s5-render — 10s

- **Technique:** traveling connector — a path draws through three quality-check icons.
- **Primary visual:** `Sparkle Action.png` (motion) leads; a single connector
  path draws rightward through `Ribbon.png` (brand) and `Hat Graduation.png`
  (instructional quality) and `Shield Checkmark.png` (trust), each icon
  settling as the path reaches it, timed to "clear, consistent, and
  trustworthy."
- **Layout:** full-width strip, left-to-right motivated travel.
- **Narration:** Line 5 (10s).
- **Caption:** on.

## s6-outcome — 13.5s

- **Technique:** compress-then-expand field — pipeline loop shrinks, then a
  field of lesson icons reaches team icons.
- **Primary visual:** `Document Text.png` travels to `Filmstrip Play.png` as
  one small looped path (the repeatable pipeline); the video icon then
  multiplies into a sequential field of small `Video.png` tiles reaching
  `People Team.png`; field clears to the closing distilled line "Visual
  learning. Faster. Still rigorous." held still.
- **Layout:** centered loop → distributed field → centered type hold.
- **Narration:** Line 6 (13.5s).
- **Caption:** on.

## s7-endcard — 2.5s

- **Technique:** still title lockup, single reveal, held.
- **Primary visual:** Text-only "Microsoft 365 Copilot" lockup on the brand
  accent color; no logo mark is fabricated (no verified brand asset was
  available locally), single restrained reveal, then a static hold for the
  remainder.
- **Narration:** none (silent).
- **Caption:** off (no narration to caption).
