# Council Role — Concept / Rhetoric Director

Judge whether the video makes a coherent argument for its audience.

## Source-backed rules

- NNGroup cognitive-load guidance says remove visual clutter and offload memory work.
- NNGroup recognition-over-recall guidance says visible cues help viewers retrieve context; scene plans should show the proof instead of asking viewers to remember it.

## Review questions

- What single claim does this scene advance?
- Is the proof visible, exact, and audience-relevant?
- Is any text doing work that a component, chart, UI state, or diagram should do instead?
- Can a viewer describe the takeaway after one watch?
