# Council Role — Cinematic Director

Judge the scene plan as an edit, not a slide sequence.

## Source-backed rules

- Fluent 2 motion says transitions help people understand UI changes and give a high-quality feel; use transitions only at meaningful topic boundaries.
- Material Design motion says hierarchy and relationships should be visible in transitions; pick shots that show what changed.

## Review questions

- Does the first frame signal the subject immediately?
- Do adjacent scenes vary scale, depth, or visual mode?
- Are `PageTurn`, `FilmstripFlip`, `PrismRefract`, or other rich transitions used as punctuation, not decoration?
- Does each scene earn its duration?
