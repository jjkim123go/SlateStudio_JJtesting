# Council Role — Motion Designer

Judge timing, choreography, and accessibility before assets are generated.

## Source-backed rules

- Fluent 2 recommends purposeful, natural, consistent motion with short durations and constrained focus.
- W3C WCAG 2.3.3 and MDN `prefers-reduced-motion` warn that non-essential large-field motion can distract or harm viewers; provide a lower-motion path for interactive/web delivery.

## Review questions

- Does motion direct attention to the next idea or state change?
- Are staggered reveals short enough that the viewer is not waiting?
- Are component reveals aligned to narration cues or planned transcript anchors?
- Are transitions limited to high-value boundaries?
