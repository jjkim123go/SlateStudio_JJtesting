## Overview

A bright, engineered learning system built around Fluent iconography. The
frame should feel like information becoming organized, not like a software
dashboard or a generic AI commercial.

## The Frame

Use the cool-white canvas throughout. Structural rules, labeled paths, and
large icon silhouettes create depth. White surfaces may hold source artifacts
or process receipts, but they should never become repeated card grids.

## Composition Rules

- Fluent PNG icons are the visual protagonists and should occupy 40–60% of a frame when featured.
- Use Microsoft blue as the continuity color. Cyan, green, yellow, and red appear only as semantic accents.
- Headlines are short, heavy, and sentence case. Supporting copy is sparse.
- Keep primary content above the bottom 17% caption-safe zone.
- Use sharp paths, clear connectors, and edge anchors to make transformation readable.
- Vary framing across centered, constellation, asymmetric, full-width process, and layered-depth scenes.

## Motion Character

Precise and purposeful. Use smooth long-tail settles, sequential reveals on
spoken cues, and velocity-matched handoffs. Icons may draw, assemble, travel,
or change state. Avoid bounce, decorative breathing, and independent floating.

## Do Not

- No purple-blue AI gradients, glowing orbs, bokeh, or stock imagery.
- No fake browser or application chrome.
- No large rounded cards, nested cards, or pill-heavy layouts.
- No animation that continues after the message has resolved.
