# SCRIPT — Knowledge-to-Video Factory

**Voice:** Concise English narrator; provider and voice selected after storyboard approval
**Voice settings:** Natural pace, approximately 2.1 words per second
**Voice direction:** Clear, confident, technically literate, and instructional rather than promotional.

---

## Line 1 — The possibility (Frame 1)

**Time:** 0.0 – 6.5s
**Delivery:** Open with curiosity; let "see and understand" land.

    What if every page of trusted knowledge could become a lesson people can see and understand?

## Line 2 — Name the factory (Frame 2)

**Time:** 6.5 – 15.0s
**Delivery:** Define the idea plainly, with emphasis on the three source types.

    The Knowledge-to-Video Factory turns Microsoft Learn content, technical documentation, and support articles into conceptual video experiences.

## Line 3 — Analyze with agents (Frame 3)

**Time:** 15.0 – 24.0s
**Delivery:** Precise and reassuring; stress accuracy.

    Specialized AI agents read the source, identify the teaching spine, and protect the claims that must stay accurate.

## Line 4 — Build the learning plan (Frame 4)

**Time:** 24.0 – 34.0s
**Delivery:** Build momentum through the three ordered outputs.

    Then they work in sequence: shaping the script, mapping a visual storyboard, and preparing narration for each beat.

## Line 5 — Render with rigor (Frame 5)

**Time:** 34.0 – 44.0s
**Delivery:** Confident; give "clear, consistent, and trustworthy" equal weight.

    HyperFrames turns that plan into motion, while brand and instructional checks keep every scene clear, consistent, and trustworthy.

## Line 6 — The outcome (Frame 6)

**Time:** 44.0 – 57.5s
**Delivery:** Resolve warmly, slowing slightly on the final clause.

    The result is a repeatable path from written knowledge to visual learning, helping teams explain complex ideas faster, without losing rigor.
