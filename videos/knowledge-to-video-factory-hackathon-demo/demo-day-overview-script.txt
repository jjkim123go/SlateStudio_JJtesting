Technical knowledge is valuable, but turning documentation into engaging video content can be difficult and time-consuming.
This project explores how agentic AI can help transform trusted technical content into scripts, storyboards, and video-ready learning experiences.
Starting with existing documentation, the workflow identifies key concepts and helps transform them into a structured visual narrative. Motion and visual storytelling can reveal relationships and processes that are often difficult to communicate through text alone.
Rather than replacing creators, the goal is to reduce repetitive production tasks and help teams focus on storytelling, quality, creativity, and learning outcomes.
Throughout this hackathon, we explored how AI-assisted workflows can support the creation of consistent, scalable learning content while keeping humans in control of accuracy, messaging, and creative direction.
This project represents an early exploration of how agentic AI can help make technical knowledge more accessible, engaging, and easier to understand through video.
