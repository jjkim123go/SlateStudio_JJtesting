# Knowledge-to-Video Factory Hackathon Demo

- Audience: Hackathon judges and Microsoft technical/content stakeholders
- Runtime: 110 seconds total; 107.4 seconds narrated
- Format: 1920x1080, 30 fps
- Workflow: Direct HyperFrames HTML and GSAP
- Music: None

## Intent

Demonstrate how agentic AI can reduce the production barrier between existing
Microsoft knowledge and effective learning video, while clearly positioning the
system as an accelerator for human creators rather than a replacement.

## Visual Direction

Use `hyperframes-projects/microsoft-copilot-architecture/
microsoft-copilot-architecture-editorial.mp4` as the visual and motion
reference only. Keep this project's Hackathon narration and factual content.

Follow the reference's restrained editorial system: a persistent left column
for the product title and current narration passage, a large deterministic
diagram stage on the right, soft white paper texture, Segoe UI typography,
compact cards, thin routed connectors, and sparse semantic color. Use selective
camera pullbacks within scenes and approximately 0.55-second chapter fades.

No generated imagery, generic architecture decoration, fake application UI, or
scene-heading overlays. Motion follows the narration through path travel,
sequential assembly, and long-tail settles.
